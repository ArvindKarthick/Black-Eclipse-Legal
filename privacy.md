# BLACK ECLIPSE
## PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** November 11, 2026

Black Eclipse ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored and how it is protected when you interact with the Black Eclipse Discord Bot, website and related services.

By using Black Eclipse, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide and maintain our services, Black Eclipse may collect and store the following information:

**1.1 Discord Account Information**
- Discord User ID.
- Discord server and guild IDs where required for server-specific features.
- Discord usernames and display names when needed for displaying user information.

**1.2 Economy Information**
- Global Crystal wallet balances.
- Bank balances and banking information.
- Transaction and transfer records, where maintained.
- Loan records and repayment information.
- Daily, weekly and other reward claim records.
- Shop purchases and role-related records.

**1.3 Game Information**
- Gambling game activity and results.
- Betting amounts and winnings.
- Game statistics and leaderboard information.
- Other records required to operate game features.

**1.4 Investment Information**
- Virtual stock ownership.
- Stock purchase and sale records.
- Simulated market activity.
- Investment balances and related statistics.

**1.5 Technical Information**
- Command usage and interaction records when logged.
- Error reports and diagnostic information.
- Information necessary to prevent abuse, investigate bugs and maintain service reliability.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by Black Eclipse is used for the following purposes:

- Creating and maintaining user economy accounts.
- Managing global Crystal balances.
- Processing deposits, withdrawals and transfers.
- Operating banking and loan features.
- Processing game bets, rewards and results.
- Maintaining simulated stock market records.
- Displaying leaderboards and user statistics.
- Assigning and managing squad and shop roles.
- Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized activities.
- Troubleshooting technical issues.
- Improving bot performance and reliability.

We use information only for legitimate service-related purposes.

### 3. Global Crystal Economy

Black Eclipse uses a global Crystal economy.

Your Crystal wallet balance is associated with your Discord User ID and may be accessible across participating servers where Black Eclipse operates.

This means your wallet balance is not necessarily isolated to one Discord server.

Banking, investment and other economy records may also be associated with your account to maintain consistent service functionality.

### 4. Data Storage and Security

Black Eclipse uses database and hosting infrastructure to store and process information required for its operation.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no online service or database can guarantee absolute security.

Access to stored information is restricted to authorized personnel who require it for maintenance, administration or security purposes.

### 5. Data Sharing

Black Eclipse does not sell users' personal information.

We do not share user information with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Black Eclipse, including:

- Discord, for account identification and bot functionality.
- Database providers, for storing economy and account records.
- Hosting providers, for operating the bot and website.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of Black Eclipse and its users.

### 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Black Eclipse services.

Some records may remain stored even when a user stops interacting with the bot, particularly when they are necessary for maintaining the global economy, preventing abuse or resolving disputes.

Data may be corrected, archived or deleted when appropriate and technically feasible.

### 7. User Data Requests

Users may contact the Black Eclipse administration team to request information about data associated with their Discord account.

Depending on the nature of the request, users may request:

- Access to eligible stored account information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion of eligible personal information.
- Clarification regarding how their information is used.

Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security or legal purposes.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable laws.

### 8. Cookies and Website Information

If you access a Black Eclipse website, certain technical information may be processed to maintain website functionality and security.

Cookies or similar technologies may be used if required by website features or integrations.

Any additional analytics or tracking technologies, if introduced, should be disclosed through an updated Privacy Policy.

### 9. Children's Privacy

Black Eclipse is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and all applicable age-related laws when using the service.

### 10. Third-Party Services

Black Eclipse relies on third-party platforms and infrastructure, including Discord, hosting providers and database services.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of these services.

Users should review the relevant third-party privacy policies when using their services.

### 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, features, data practices or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published through our official Black Eclipse platforms.

The revised policy becomes effective when published unless otherwise stated.

### 12. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy-related questions, data access requests, corrections or deletion requests, users may contact the Black Eclipse administration team through the official Black Eclipse Discord community.

Please include your Discord User ID when submitting an account-related request so that the relevant records can be identified.

### 13. Your Agreement

By using Black Eclipse, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
